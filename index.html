The Last Train

You just missed your train. The sign says the next one is twenty minutes away, and your phone is at 2%. You sit down and notice a black backpack under the bench.

Before you can decide what to do, you hear someone knock on the glass of the station office. At the same time, a train pulls in. Its lights are on, but you cannot see anyone inside.

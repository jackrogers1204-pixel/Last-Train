The Empty Car

You step into the car just before the doors close. There is one other passenger at the far end. She is searching every pocket of her jacket.

"Did you see a backpack at the last station?" she asks. "My project is in there. I have to present it tomorrow." You tell her it was under a bench. At the next stop, she gets up to go back. You could help her, but you are already tired and almost home.

What do you do?

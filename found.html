The Right Stop

You bring the backpack to the window. A few minutes later, the student runs down the stairs. She opens the bag and pulls out a cardboard model of the city. One tiny building has fallen over, but the rest is fine.

She thanks you about five times. The worker tells you another train is coming in four minutes. You sit back down, glad you did not spend the whole night wondering whose bag it was.
